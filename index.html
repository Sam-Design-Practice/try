Your monthly support means we can say “yes” when someone reaches out.

If you need help, you are welcome here.

We know asking for help can feel hard. We keep it simple and respectful.

1. Get referred

Speak to your GP, social worker, school, advice centre or one of our partners — they’ll refer you to us if you’re in crisis.

  • Income drop
  • Unexpected bills
  • Family/health issues

2. Collect your parcel

You’ll get 3 days’ worth of food and essential items. Friendly volunteers will be there to help you.

Tell us about dietary needs when you arrive.

3. Get further support

We’ll connect you to Foyle Network services — debt, benefits, housing, wellbeing, family support.

You’re not just getting food — you’re getting a way forward.
